NENASIMON

Food knowledge travels with people.

I work with food as a form of knowledge: something carried through families, communities and places, and continually changed through practice.

My work brings together cooking, sake, fermentation and education, with a particular interest in tacit and embodied knowledge — the things we learn by doing, tasting, making and sharing.

01 / COOKING

Cooking

Food practitioner and cook working with Japanese home cooking, seasonal ingredients and fermentation.

Cooking as practice

I cook professionally for private events, catering and lunch services, while continuing to explore everyday Japanese food practices and sourdough baking.

Selected work

Greece — food residency, 2024

During a two-and-a-half-week residency in Greece, I worked with local cooks and ingredients in Halkidiki and Thessaloniki.

Rather than making “Japanese-Greek fusion”, I brought what I know into conversation with what was already there: local rice, sardines, sesame, herbs, coastal plants, lavender and mulberry leaves.

I adapted Japanese techniques and ingredients to the place, while learning from local cooks and the landscape through cooking, foraging and shared meals.

Current cooking work

Party catering, lunch services, collaborative food events and sourdough baking. Available for selected collaborations in Finland and internationally.

02 / SAKE

Kikizakeshi

Certified sake sommelier working with sake as part of a living food tradition.

Sake as food knowledge

My sake work is about more than tasting notes. I am interested in the people, ingredients, techniques and cultural practices behind sake, and in creating ways for people to encounter it through food.

Services

What I do

Sake consulting, pairing, tastings, guest sommelier work, restaurant and importer collaborations, and professional training.

Collaborations

Available for dinners, pop-ups, tasting events, cultural projects and selected international collaborations.

03 / EDUCATION

Education

Sharing food knowledge through workshops, making and conversation.

Learning through doing

I create workshops around Japanese home cooking, fermentation and food culture, focusing on practical knowledge that can be experienced rather than simply explained.

Workshops

Japanese home cooking

Workshops exploring everyday Japanese cooking practices, ingredients, preservation and ways of working with what is available.

Fermentation

Hands-on sessions exploring fermentation as both a universal process and a local practice, including miso and other traditional food techniques.

Food culture & knowledge

Sessions for groups, cultural organisations and food communities interested in food knowledge, cultural transmission and embodied practice.

Co-founder and organiser of a shared food business infrastructure for small food entrepreneurs in Finland.

Building shared infrastructure

At Zestii Kitchens, I work with a community of food entrepreneurs to build shared spaces, resources and opportunities for a more diverse, human-scale food economy.

My role

ABOUT

One practice, many forms.

Cooking, sake, education and community organising are different expressions of the same interest: how food knowledge is carried, embodied and passed on.

My work is influenced by Slow Food and by the idea that food is not only a product, but also a way of connecting people, places, knowledge and livelihoods.

I am particularly interested in tacit and embodied knowledge: knowledge held in hands, senses, routines, recipes, fermentation practices and shared meals.

I work from Helsinki and across Finland, with selected international collaborations and residencies.
